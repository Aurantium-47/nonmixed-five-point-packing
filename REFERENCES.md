# Sources and attribution

## Mathematical source

Alexander Natalchenko and Arsenii Sagdeev. *Packing Density of Sets With Only Two Nonmixed Gaps*. Journal of Graph Theory **110** (2025), no. 3, 263–271. [DOI: 10.1002/jgt.23269](https://doi.org/10.1002/jgt.23269). The version consulted is [arXiv:2407.01101v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01101v2), revised June 6, 2025. The [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01101) supplies the journal reference and version history.

The following locations refer to the pagination of the arXiv v2 PDF:

- Page 1, footnote 1: symmetric upper density on $[-N,N]$.
- Page 2: packing center sets, the forbidden-difference reformulation, Theorem 2, and the unnumbered sharpness conjecture immediately following it.
- Pages 3–4, Section 2: the periodic lower-bound construction, specialized and proved in the present note.
- Page 2, Theorem 3: the cases $k=1$ or $m=1$ proved in the source.
- Pages 4–6, Section 3.1: the source's local upper-bound framework. The present note supplies its own path identity, equality argument, and interval decomposition, so that its upper-bound proof can be read without importing this framework.

The theorem of the present note uses exactly $k=m=2$. It is distinct from the source's numbered Conjecture 1. The periodic lower constructions and the zero-remainder equality are credited to the source throughout. No publication-priority claim is made, and this bibliography is not an exhaustive priority survey.

## Nature of the proof

The upper bound is proved directly from a finite path identity, integer counting, and an explicit symmetric-interval estimate. The lower-bound proof uses Euclid's divisibility lemma for coprime integers and Euclidean division with remainder in $\{0,1,2,3,4\}$. No external periodic-maximizer theorem, finite-state certificate, numerical approximation, or formalization result is assumed by the mathematical proof.
